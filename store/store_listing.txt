FULL DESCRIPTION:
🧪 POUR. SORT. PERFECT. 🧪

Welcome to the lab! Every test tube is a scrambled mess of glowing orbs, and only you can fix it. Tap a tube to lift its top orbs, tap another to pour — but orbs only land on a matching color or empty glass. Sort every tube until each one glows a single pure color.

⚗️ HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a tube — its top orbs rise above the rim
• Tap another tube to pour them in
• Orbs only pour onto the SAME color or into empty space
• Matching orbs pour together in one move
• Make every tube one pure color to win!

✨ FEATURES
• 150 puzzles: Easy (4–6 colors), Medium (7–8), Hard (9–10)
• Every single level is verified solvable by a built-in solver — no impossible deals, ever
• Smart par system: the solver's own solution sets the target — beat it for 3 stars
• Unlimited undo for fearless experimenting
• Gorgeous glassy tubes, glowing orbs and a bubbling lab ambiance
• Relaxing ambient soundtrack
• Haptic feedback
• 100% offline, no ads, no purchases, zero data collection
